## CCTV for homes

A CCTV system can provide useful visibility around a residential property.

For homeowners, cameras may be useful for monitoring areas such as entrances, gates, driveways, compounds and other important points around the property.

Every home is different, so the number and placement of cameras required will depend on the size and layout of the property and the areas you want to monitor.

If you are not sure what type of CCTV setup your home needs, you can tell us about the property and what you want to achieve, and we can discuss the available options with you.

## Choosing the right CCTV system

There is no single CCTV setup that is right for every property.

The appropriate system depends on several factors, including:

- The size and layout of the property
- The number of areas that need monitoring
- Indoor and outdoor requirements
- Lighting conditions
- Distance between cameras and recording equipment
- Whether remote viewing is required
- How much footage needs to be stored
- Existing network infrastructure
- Your budget and priorities

For this reason, choosing a CCTV system should involve more than simply counting the number of cameras.

The aim is to create a setup that provides useful coverage of the areas that matter most to you.

---

## Understanding the main parts of a CCTV system

A CCTV system typically consists of several components that work together.

### Cameras

Cameras capture the video that forms the basis of the surveillance system.

Different cameras and configurations may be appropriate for different locations and lighting conditions.

### DVR or NVR

The recording device stores and manages footage from the cameras.

Depending on the system, this may be a DVR or an NVR.

### Storage

Recorded footage needs somewhere to be stored. Storage capacity affects how much footage can be retained before older recordings are overwritten.

### Cabling and connections

Cameras need appropriate connections for power, video and/or network communication depending on the type of system being installed.

### Monitor or display

A display can be connected to the recording system so that live and recorded footage can be viewed locally.

### Network connection

Where remote viewing or network-based functionality is required, the CCTV system may need to be connected to an appropriate network.

Each component needs to work correctly for the complete system to function as intended.

## Wired and network CCTV systems

CCTV systems can use different technologies and installation approaches.

Traditional camera systems may use dedicated cabling to connect cameras to a recording device, while network-based systems can use network infrastructure to transmit video.

The appropriate option depends on the property, existing infrastructure, equipment requirements and the type of installation.

We can help you understand the options available for your particular setup.

## Remote CCTV monitoring

Being able to view your cameras remotely can be particularly useful when you are away from your property.

Depending on the equipment and network configuration, remote access can allow you to view available camera feeds and, where supported, review recorded footage using a compatible device.

Remote viewing requires the CCTV equipment and network to be configured correctly.

If your existing CCTV system is not allowing you to view your cameras remotely, we can help troubleshoot the setup and identify the likely cause.

## When your CCTV system is not working properly

You don't necessarily need to know what is wrong before contacting us.

If your CCTV system has stopped working or is behaving unexpectedly, tell us what you are experiencing.

For example:

- One or more cameras have gone off
- The cameras show no picture
- The system is not recording
- Recorded footage cannot be accessed
- The DVR or NVR is not working properly
- The monitor shows an error
- Remote viewing has stopped working
- Cameras are intermittently disconnecting
- The system has power but no video
- The CCTV system needs general troubleshooting

We can assess the problem and discuss the appropriate next step.

## Why proper CCTV installation matters

A CCTV system is only useful when it provides the coverage and functionality you actually need.

Poor camera positioning, inadequate connections, unsuitable equipment or incorrect configuration can reduce the usefulness of a surveillance system.

Proper planning and installation help ensure that the cameras are positioned appropriately, the recording equipment is configured correctly and the system works together as intended.

That's why we look beyond simply installing cameras. We consider how the system will be used and which parts of the property are most important to monitor.
