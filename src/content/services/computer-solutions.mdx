## When should you repair or replace your computer?

Not every computer problem means you need to buy a new device.

Sometimes a repair or upgrade can restore a system to a useful condition. In other cases, continued repairs may no longer make financial or practical sense.

Factors such as the age of the computer, the nature of the fault, its specifications and how you use it can all affect the best decision.

If you're unsure whether to repair, upgrade or replace your computer, tell us what you're experiencing. We can assess the situation and discuss the available options with you.

---

## Why choose the right computer solution?

Buying or repairing a computer without considering how it will actually be used can lead to unnecessary expenses.

A computer that is suitable for basic office work may not be appropriate for demanding professional applications. Similarly, replacing a computer that only needs a simple upgrade may not be the most practical choice.

That's why we start with the requirement.

We consider what you need the computer to do, the problems you're experiencing and the available options before recommending a solution.
