faqs:
  - question: "What is access control?"
    answer: "Access control is a system used to manage who is allowed to enter a particular property, building, room or other restricted area."
  - question: "What types of access control systems do you install?"
    answer: "Depending on the requirements and available equipment, access control can use methods such as fingerprints, cards, proximity tags or keypads. The appropriate option depends on the property and how access needs to be managed."
  - question: "Can access control be installed in a home?"
    answer: "Yes. Access control can be used for selected entrances and areas in residential properties."
  - question: "Can access control be installed in an office?"
    answer: "Yes. Businesses can use access control to manage entry to offices, restricted rooms and other areas where controlled access is required."
  - question: "Can access control be used in schools?"
    answer: "Yes. Schools and educational institutions can use access control for selected entrances, administrative areas, staff rooms and other restricted locations."
  - question: "What happens if an access card stops working?"
    answer: "There can be several possible causes, including the card, reader, configuration or another part of the system. We can help troubleshoot the problem."
  - question: "What if the fingerprint reader doesn't recognise a user?"
    answer: "The issue may be related to the user's registration, the reader, environmental conditions or another part of the system. The system can be assessed to determine the likely cause."
  - question: "Can you repair an existing access control system?"
    answer: "Yes. We can help troubleshoot existing access control systems and identify problems with readers, locks, power, connections and other components."
  - question: "Do I need to replace my entire system if one component fails?"
    answer: "Not necessarily. Depending on the equipment and the nature of the problem, it may be possible to replace or repair only the affected component."
  - question: "Can access control work together with CCTV?"
    answer: "Yes. Access control and CCTV can complement each other, with access control managing entry and CCTV providing visual monitoring."
  - question: "Can access control work with an intercom?"
    answer: "Depending on the equipment and setup, access control and intercom systems can be used together as part of an entrance management solution."
  - question: "Can you add another door to my existing access control system?"
    answer: "This depends on the existing equipment and its capabilities. We can assess the current system and determine what options are available."
  - question: "Can you help me choose an access control system?"
    answer: "Yes. Tell us about your property, the entrances you want to control and how you want people to gain access. We can discuss the available options."
